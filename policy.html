Policy

The whole policy, in plain language.

This is the deal, written the way we talk everywhere else. The lawyered version will be published before launch, and it will say the same thing, just longer.

What we keep

Your verified parent status, each kid's age band, and the approvals you have made. That is the whole working set, and you can see every bit of it in the app.

What we never keep

Names, birthdays, faces, IDs, schools, locations, browsing, messages. Verification documents are checked, matched, and deleted; we keep the result, never the document.

What sites get

An age band and your answer, signed so they can verify it. That is all a site can request, and if one asks us for more, the answer is no.

Taking it back

Revoke any approval and it stops working on the site's next check. Delete your account and the family's records go with it.

Guardian is pre-launch: nothing here handles real family data yet, and this page will carry the effective date once it does.
