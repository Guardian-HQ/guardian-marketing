For platforms

Integration details are coming soon.

Guardian answers one question for a platform: did a verified parent approve this, for this child, right now. The answer is a signed credential your service checks locally, carrying an age band, the permissions granted, any limits, and a pseudonym that is yours alone. No name, no birthday, no document ever reaches you.

The developer guide, sandbox access, and the verification library are being prepared for the first pilot partners.

To talk about a pilot, write to contact@regoguardian.com.
