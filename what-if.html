A thought experiment

What if every app had to ask you first?

What if the platform had to get your permission before your 11-year-old could use voice chat?

Not a setting buried in a menu you have to find. A request, sent to you, before the thing happens.

What if that were true for every app, not just the ones that decided to build it?

Before an AI companion. Before location sharing. Before a purchase. Before a stranger can send them a message.

What if you could change your mind instantly?

Not a support ticket. Not a 30-day policy window. A tap, and the access is gone on the platform's next check.

What if the platform never needed to know who your child is to know the answer?

No name. No birthday. No face. No document. The platform gets an answer, and nothing else.

That's Guardian.

One private place to approve, limit, or revoke what your child can do online, that works the same way on every platform that asks.
